CRITICAL EXTRACTION RULES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. Extract ONLY facts explicitly stated in the SOURCE TEXT at the end of this prompt.
2. NEVER invent information from your training data. Every name, tag, number and unit you output must
   appear literally in the source text.
3. Every entity, relationship and claim MUST include "evidence": copy the COMPLETE source sentence
   (or the complete table row) that supports it, not a fragment. Add "sentence_index" (0-based index of
   that sentence/line in the source text) when you can.
4. If the source text does not mention it, DO NOT extract it. If nothing is extractable, return empty arrays.
5. Use glossary terms exactly as defined — do NOT re-interpret abbreviations.
6. Extract EVERY routing, containment, control and protection statement as a relationship.
   Elliptical forms count:
     "X to Y" / "X is routed to Y"       -> X FEEDS Y (or X DISCHARGES_TO Y for lines/headers)
     "X from Y"                            -> X RECEIVES_FROM Y
     "X upstream of TAG" / "downstream of" -> X UPSTREAM_OF TAG / X DOWNSTREAM_OF TAG
     "A comprises B" / "A consists of B"   -> B PART_OF A
     "X is protected by Y"                 -> X PROTECTED_BY Y
     "X is controlled by Y"                -> X CONTROLLED_BY Y
     "X takes suction from Y"              -> X SUCTION_FROM Y
     "X is driven by a motor/turbine"      -> X DRIVEN_BY motor/turbine
   A routing list ("Kerosene to" followed by "Storage", "Diesel Header") gives one relationship per line.
7. Tables: the pipeline converts every numeric table cell into a claim deterministically, so do NOT
   list table cells as claims. From a table extract the ENTITIES it names (products, streams, crudes,
   equipment tags) and any relationship its rows state. Claims are for numbers stated in prose sentences.
8. Report a confidence between 0 and 1; use lower confidence rather than omitting a stated fact.
9. Do NOT assign general engineering knowledge as if it came from this document.
10. Every prose claim MUST say WHICH value it is. Fill "parameter_role" (design | rated | normal | operating |
    minimum | maximum | mechanical_design | test | relief | alarm | trip), "location" (suction | discharge |
    inlet | outlet | top | bottom | overhead | flash_zone | shell | tube ...), "operating_mode" (normal_operation |
    startup | shutdown | emergency | temporary | upset | commissioning), "scenario" (crude/design case or mode
    named in the text: "Basrah", "Bombay High", "BH mode", "PG mode", "SKO operation" ...) and
    "temporal_status" (current | historical | design | defunct) whenever the text states them; leave "" when it
    does not. "Design capacity 3.0 MMTPA" and "enhanced to 3.2 MMTPA" are two different facts (design vs
    rated/current), not a contradiction; "normal flow 482" and "minimum flow 219" likewise.
11. A facility that "was provided" but is "now defunct" / "not in use" is one claim with temporal_status
    "defunct" (and the original purpose as evidence), never two contradictory claims.
